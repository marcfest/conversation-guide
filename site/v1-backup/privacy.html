Privacy

Effective date: June 2026  ·  conversation.guide

The short version: we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you type stays on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. Conversation Guide does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party data services.

Local storage

Your browser's localStorage is used solely to keep your own work for you between visits. Specifically, we store on your device:

  • Your prepared answers to the four questions, and
  • Your mental movie note.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server. You can erase it any time with the "Clear" button, or by clearing your browser's site data for conversation.guide.

No analytics

We do not use Google Analytics, Plausible, Mixpanel, or any other analytics platform. We do not track page views, sessions, or behavior.

No cookies

We set no cookies — not for tracking, not for preferences, not for any purpose.

Offline use

This app installs a service worker so it can work offline once loaded. The cache holds only the app's own files (this page and its icons) on your device. It transmits nothing about you.

Children's privacy

This tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy meaningfully, we will update the effective date above. Since we collect no data, any change can only expand, not reduce, your privacy.

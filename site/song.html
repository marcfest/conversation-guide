The song

A song travels well. It turns up on its own, while you are driving or washing up, which is close to when you would actually want these questions.

So the four questions are a song. It is 57 seconds long. The chorus is only four words, and each one is the stressed word inside its question.

This version was made with an AI music tool, to find out whether the idea works before asking a musician to spend real craft on something untested.

The Four Questions (0:57)

Download

Here are four questions that can help in hard conversations.

What do you think?
What's shaped your thinking on that?
How are you feeling about it?
And what would make it better?

Here's the whole thing in four words.

Think. Shaped. Feeling. Better.

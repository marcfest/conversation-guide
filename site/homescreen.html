Add to Home Screen

Keep the four questions one tap away. It installs like a normal app and works offline.

 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an up-arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

▶ Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the menu ⋮ in the top-right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Confirm.

🖥 Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Click the install icon in the address bar, or open the menu ⋮.
  2. Choose Install.
